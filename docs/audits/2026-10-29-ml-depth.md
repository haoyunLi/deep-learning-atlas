## Legacy improvements worth making

These are specific teaching gaps or overbroad phrases, not evidence that the entire corresponding lesson is wrong.

1. **VAE / `lessons.ts`**: the tuning bullet “调 KL 权重 β” needs an explicit qualification that β≠1 changes the standard ELBO objective for the original model. The displayed prior KL is not the posterior-approximation gap. Link to `variational-inference-elbo` and add the binary numerical identity.
2. **Deep Ensembles / `frontierSystemsExpansion.ts`**: “M 从 3–5 常能看到大部分收益” sounds like a broad empirical guarantee without task-specific evidence. Prefer “从 M=3/5 作预算起点，画实际收益曲线”. Clarify that entropy-difference/MI under a finite ensemble is a disagreement statistic; independently optimized members are not automatically posterior samples, and this is not a calibrated decomposition of all real-world uncertainty.
3. **Conformal / `frontierSystemsExpansion.ts`**: the current marginal-coverage caveat is already correct. Add the distinct caveat that a 90% set-coverage guarantee does not directly imply 10% error among accepted singleton predictions. Report selective risk versus acceptance rate separately; reject cost/capacity belongs to decision design.
4. **Class Imbalance / `dataConcepts.ts`**: the existing probability-calibration caveat is correct but qualitative. Add the concrete weighted-Log-Loss result r=.2, positive weight3 → optimal report3/7, then link to Proper Scoring and Bayes Decision Theory. Avoid presenting class weighting and cost-optimal thresholding as automatically interchangeable under finite training/capacity.
5. **Domain Shift / `dataConcepts.ts`**: retain its definitions and add a mechanism link: source prior .2→target .5 changes posterior .4→8/11 only under Label Shift assumptions. Unlabelled drift detection alone cannot establish that P(X|Y) is stable.
6. **Bayesian Updating / `mechanismExpansion.ts`**: the current hierarchy caveat is correct; add a link to the concrete pooling lesson. Repeating group observations improves within-group estimates, while population hyperparameter uncertainty remains governed by group diversity and model assumptions.

## Figure and interaction recommendations

Every added lesson has a three-stage numerical figure, mechanism exercise and decision/debug exercise. The strongest additional controls would be: (a) group n and τ for shrinkage, (b) FP/FN/review costs for expected-risk curves, (c) τ and q for Pinball slopes and flat minima, (d) q probabilities versus ELBO/gap. Use exact toy controls with stated assumptions; do not show arbitrary animated curves disconnected from the displayed equations.

Root's HMM/Kalman labs complement this work by connecting posterior state estimation and predictive uncertainty to time, rather than duplicating these six lessons.

## Primary sources checked online

- [Blei et al., Variational Inference review](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00670): ELBO identity, reverse KL, Mean-field family, approximation tradeoffs.
- [Stan User’s Guide, Hierarchical regression](https://mc-stan.org/docs/stan-users-guide/regression.html#hierarchical-regression): pooled/no-pooling/partial pooling distinction, hierarchical scale priors.
- [Gneiting & Raftery, Strictly Proper Scoring Rules](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf): propriety, calibration/sharpness, Brier and logarithmic score; lessons use lower-is-better loss convention.
- [scikit-learn, Decision threshold tuning](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/classification_threshold.html): probabilities versus action thresholds and split discipline.
- [scikit-learn, QuantileRegressor](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.QuantileRegressor.html): conditional quantiles, Pinball Loss, L1 regularization.
- [Lipton et al., BBSE](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/lipton18a.html): Label Shift assumptions and invertible-confusion-matrix identification; posterior correction arithmetic is explicitly derived in the lesson.
